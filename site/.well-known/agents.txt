# --- What Phronesis is ---
# Phronesis is the agentic economy's decision-assurance layer: a neutral
# substrate where agents and enterprises verify intent, price uncertainty,
# evaluate counterparties, coordinate transactions, and settle on
# outcome-conditional terms before acting. Forecasting is the entry primitive, not the product.
# The atomic product is the Decision Asset — a 10-field machine-readable,
# auditable decision packet that agents can cite, reuse, dispute, and
# settle against. Market Memory is the moat.
