Frequently asked questions

What exactly am I paying for?

Reading the record is free. The paid plans are not live: until they are, a subscription adds nothing to Discovery. The Decision Room is priced per engagement.

Can I switch tiers mid-month?

Yes — write to support and we'll switch you; the change is prorated.

What payment methods?

Stripe (cards). Agent-native x402 payment: in build; not yet accepted.

What is the Discovery rate limit?

100 API reads per rolling 30 days; public pages are unlimited.

How does the Decision Room work?

Every inquiry is reviewed by a person. If it fits, we offer a thirty-minute call. The engagement is milestone-priced and credited against your first year of subscription.

Where is your calibration evidence?

The public scorecard at /scorecard. Phronesis issues no calibration grade today. A grade will require at least thirty scored records under any proper scoring rule, with calibration stated per rule family. Until then the scorecard shows the resolved and scored counts and each record's score. Where a number isn't earned, the page shows a blank we can defend.
