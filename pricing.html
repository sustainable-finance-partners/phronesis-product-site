Rails

Humans pay by card through Stripe. Agents acting for a human will pay through Stripe's agent checkout when per-request purchases are live. Agent-native payment over x402 (USDC on Base) is in preparation; not yet accepted — and every priced request will offer it alongside Stripe when it is live. Phronesis holds no customer keys; receipts settle to an operator account.

Re-reads and retries

When per-request purchases are live (in build): A purchase buys a version of a Decision Asset, identified by its digest. Re-reading what you bought is free until the asset is superseded, and the record tells you why it was. If your agent retries a purchase with the same purchase_id within 72 hours, nothing is charged twice.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly am I paying for?

A Decision Asset — a forecast with its evidence, a frozen resolution rule, and a public score when reality resolves. When the mint path is live (in build), you pay to mint it and a smaller amount when it resolves; reading is free or cents.

Can I switch tiers mid-month?

Yes — write to support and we'll switch you; the change is prorated.

Do you offer annual pricing?

Not yet.

What payment methods?

Stripe (cards). Agent-native x402 payment: in preparation; not yet accepted.

What is the Discovery rate limit?

100 API reads per rolling 30 days; public pages are unlimited.

How does the Decision Room work?

A ~30-minute inquiry call defines the decision, the evidence and the resolution event; the engagement is milestone-priced and credited against your first year of subscription.

Where is your calibration evidence?

The public scorecard at /scorecard. Two records have resolved; calibration grades appear when a series reaches ten resolutions. Where a number isn't earned, the page shows a blank we can defend.
